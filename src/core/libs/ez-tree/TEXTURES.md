# EZ-Tree textures

The leaf textures are from [dgreenheck/ez-tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree) and are covered by the vendored library's MIT license in `LICENSE`.

The bark textures are bundled in the EZ-Tree package. Its source notes identify Birch and Pine as [TextureCan](https://www.texturecan.com/terms/) textures, and Oak and Willow as [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) textures. Both sources publish these assets under CC0.
