# Larch House

Import `luxury_log_cabin.hyp` into the Hyperfy build that supports native `prim` extrusions. The editable application source is `luxury_log_cabin.js`.

Open `luxury_log_cabin_preview.html` in a browser for a self-contained, interactive preview. It embeds the exact delivered `.hyp` bytes and this checkout's actual primitive constructor and geometry code. The camera buttons include exterior, four elevations, entrance, mezzanine, stairs and a roof-off plan. Studio lighting and the background ground plane belong only to the preview. The preview renders doors in their initial closed state; interaction and physics require the Hyperfy runtime.

## Architecture

- Log-wall footprint: 18 × 15 m; roof overhangs reach approximately 19.9 × 17 m.
- Ground contact: Y=0; finished main floor: Y=0.12 m; mezzanine: Y=3.30 m.
- Wall/eave line: Y=6 m; roof ridge: Y=9.8 m; chimney cap: Y=11.265 m.
- The approved roof compromise preserves the requested ridge height with a 22.9° gable.
- Three rear bedrooms, each approximately 5.6–5.8 × 2.9 m clear, with 2.61 m ceiling clearance, exterior windows and real door openings.
- Approximately 39% of the footprint has a second floor. The central/front great room stays open to the exposed roof.
- A 4.6 m stone fireplace has an actual recessed arch, projecting hearth, timber mantel, tapered extruded chimney and a flashed roof penetration.
- L staircase: 18 risers of 176.7 mm, 320 mm going, nominal 1.35 m tread width and a landing. Stringers, treads and rails share one path.
- Five animated wooden doors: two entrance leaves and three bedroom doors. Server-authoritative state, client interaction actions, and kinematic leaf collision; no furniture or fire.

Updated from `/Users/jerome/Downloads/Larch House — Luxury Log Cabin.hyp` (version 13). The structural collision shell includes floors, stairs, walls, balcony guards, fireplace and both sloped roof panels. Detached colliders follow cabin transforms and are removed on destruction. Cabin-wide Cast shadows and Receive shadows toggles are available in the app properties; the added lights always have shadows disabled.

Interior lighting uses four warm paired-cone sconces, two upstairs and two downstairs on the side-wall posts, aimed up and down. Ten recessed exterior downlights sit beneath the eaves and gable overhangs. The 18 native spotlights illuminate surfaces, with shadows disabled.

All 1,624 visible primitives are generated by the JavaScript. There are no external meshes, images or textures. The package includes a tiny, geometry-free GLB solely for compatibility with Hyperfy loaders and the skill's packaging tools; it contains no meshes, materials or textures.

